You do not need a budget to start improving how your business shows up on Google. These five fixes are free, take under an hour combined, and are the same things we check first on every audit.

## 1. Claim and completely fill out your Google Business Profile

If you have not claimed it, do that first. It is free and takes about ten minutes. If you have, go back and check every field: opening hours, category, service area and at least five real photos. An incomplete profile ranks worse than a complete one, even with identical reviews.

## 2. Fix your homepage title tag

Open your homepage and look at the text in the browser tab. If it only shows your business name, you are wasting your most valuable piece of SEO real estate. It should say what you do and where, for example "Smith & Co Bakery: Custom Cakes in Leeds", not just "Home".

## 3. Check your mobile page speed

Run your homepage through Google's PageSpeed Insights. If your mobile score is under 50, that is very likely costing you rankings and customers, because most visitors on a slow mobile site leave before it finishes loading.

## 4. Confirm Google can actually find your pages

If you have Search Console set up, open the Pages report under Indexing. If important pages show as "Discovered, currently not indexed" or "Crawled, currently not indexed", Google has seen them and chosen not to include them yet. That is usually a sign of thin content or a technical issue worth looking into.

## 5. Ask your last five happy customers for a Google review

Not a general "please review us" post. Message five specific people directly who you know were happy. Review count and recency both matter for local ranking, and five fresh reviews often move the needle more than any on-page tweak.

None of this costs anything but time. If you work through all five and still see no movement after a few weeks, that is usually when something underneath needs a proper look, which is exactly what a [technical SEO audit](/services/technical-seo-audit/) is for.
