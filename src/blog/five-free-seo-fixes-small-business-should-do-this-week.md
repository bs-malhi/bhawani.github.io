You don't need a budget to start improving how your business shows up on Google. These five fixes are free, take under an hour combined, and are the same things I check first on every audit.

## 1. Claim and completely fill out your Google Business Profile

If you haven't claimed it, do that first — it's free and takes ten minutes. If you have, go back and check every field: hours, category, service area, and at least five real photos. An incomplete profile ranks worse than a complete one, even with identical reviews.

## 2. Fix your homepage title tag

Open your homepage and check the text in the browser tab. If it just says your business name with nothing else, you're wasting your most valuable piece of SEO real estate. It should say what you do and where — "Karachi Bakery — Custom Cakes & Desserts," not just "Home."

## 3. Check your mobile page speed

Run your homepage through Google's PageSpeed Insights. If your mobile score is under 50, that's very likely costing you rankings and customers — most visitors on a slow mobile site leave before it finishes loading.

## 4. Confirm Google can actually find your pages

If you have Search Console set up, check the Pages report under Indexing. If important pages show as "Discovered – currently not indexed" or "Crawled – currently not indexed," Google has seen them and chosen not to include them yet — usually a sign of thin content or a technical issue worth looking into.

## 5. Ask your last five happy customers for a Google review

Not a general "please review us" post — message five specific people directly who you know were happy. Review count and recency both matter for local ranking, and five fresh reviews often moves the needle more than any on-page tweak.

None of this costs anything but time. If you go through all five and still aren't seeing movement after a few weeks, that's usually when something underneath needs a proper look — which is exactly what a technical audit is for.
